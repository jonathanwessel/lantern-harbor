# Roadmap

Releases go to the play server (`npm run play`, http://localhost:5173) as they're ready. Every release
must load every older save without losing anything.

## Shipped
- **v0.2.0** Module split, Vite.
- **v0.3.0** The Living Harbor: research, auto-guide, bird lure, day/night, weather, birds, a harbor
  that grows with your upgrades, save export/import and backups.
- **v0.4.0** Fleet & Contracts: seven ship classes incl. ghost ships, tap-to-target, guiding combo,
  Harbor Contracts, Keeper's Logbook, Shipping Lanes research.

## Next

### v0.5 — Sound & Feel
- Ambient waves, gulls, fog bell, foghorn in fog, thunder; tap and catch sounds. Mute and volume settings.
- Settings: reduce motion, number format, haptics on phones.
- Balance pass on research costs, offline earnings, and the relight curve.

### v0.6 — Keepers
- Hire named keepers with traits (Night Owl, Birder, Storm Sailor) and assign them to the beam,
  the observatory or the lookout.

### v0.7 — New Shores (new theme)
- After a few relights, unlock a second harbor with its own look and rules: an Arctic fjord
  (northern lights, ice floes) or a tropical reef (coral, sudden squalls). Progress per harbor plus
  shared lumens.

### v0.8 — Seasons
- A rotating season every week (autumn gales, winter ice, spring migration) with its own visuals,
  modifiers and achievements.

### Later
- Install as an app (PWA) so it works offline and from a phone's home screen.
- Cloud save, so progress follows you without export codes.
