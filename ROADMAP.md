# Roadmap

Releases go to the play server (`npm run play`, http://localhost:5173) as they're ready. Every release
must load every older save without losing anything.

## Shipped
- **v0.2.0** Module split, Vite.
- **v0.3.0** The Living Harbor: research, auto-guide, bird lure, day/night, weather, birds, a harbor
  that grows with your upgrades, save export/import and backups.
- **v0.4.0** Fleet & Contracts: seven ship classes incl. ghost ships, tap-to-target, guiding combo,
  Harbor Contracts, Keeper's Logbook, Shipping Lanes research.
- **v0.5.0** Sound & Feel: synthesized ambience and effects, Settings, vibration, reduce motion,
  number formats, gem balance pass.
- **v0.6.0** Keepers: hire, assign to four posts, level up, traits with home-post perks.
- **v0.7.0** New Shores: Frostwatch Fjord and Sunreef Atoll, sea chart travel, per-harbor
  progress, harbor network bonus.
- **v0.8.0** Flares & Raiders: skua raids that steal light, the Flare Gun, Flare Battery research,
  Sharpshooter keepers.
- **v0.9.0** Seasons: weekly rotating seasons with rules, visuals, challenges and seasonal beams.
